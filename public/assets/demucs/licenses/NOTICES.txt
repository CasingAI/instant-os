Third-Party Music Source Separation Weights (HTDemucs 6-stem)
=============================================================

The following model weights are bundled under public/assets/demucs/models for
ONNX-based music source separation. The ONNX graph is MIT-licensed, but the
underlying neural-network weights originate from Meta's Demucs v4 release and
are made available for research / personal use only (NOT for commercial use).

HTDemucs 6-stem (htdemucs_6s)
- Source: https://huggingface.co/kramp/htdemucs-6s-webgpu-onnx
  (WebGPU-compatible constant-folded ONNX of StemSplitio/htdemucs-6s-onnx)
- Base model: https://huggingface.co/StemSplitio/htdemucs-6s-onnx
- Original weights: Meta Facebook Research "Demucs" v4 (Hybrid Transformer Demucs)
  https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs
- Author: Meta Platforms, Inc. (original weights); kramp (WebGPU ONNX export);
  StemSplitio (ONNX export)
- License: MIT (ONNX graph) — see LICENSE-MIT.txt
  IMPORTANT: original Meta weights are research / personal use only.
  See docs/demucs-model-license.md for the full provenance and restrictions.
- File: htdemucs_6s.onnx (284,797,240 bytes, fp32)
- Stems: drums, bass, other, vocals, guitar, piano
- Input/Output: mix [1,2,343980] -> stems [1,6,2,343980]
